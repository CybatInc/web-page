Process

How it works

Autonomous threat mitigation requires absolute precision. We've built an agentic loop that moves at the speed of the backbone.

01 · Ingestion

Real-time telemetry

Drop the Cybat SDK into your app as middleware — one line of code. It streams real HTTP traffic straight to our ingestion engine, no log export or org-level IAM grant required.

02 · Analysis

Gemini-powered reasoning

Autonomous agents use multimodal Gemini models to distinguish between legitimate spikes and sophisticated zero-day attacks in milliseconds.

03 · Action

Autonomous enforcement

Within 5 seconds, the platform automatically triggers Cloud Armor policies or VPC Firewall updates to isolate threats. Simultaneously, detailed Slack alerts notify your team of the detection and block.
