Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 30-day free trial work?

You get full access to Cybat AI for 30 days at no cost. Our team can help you connect your Cloudflare account or application SDK in 5 minutes so you can see real-time attacks on your dashboard and Slack before paying a cent.

What counts as an "API Request"?

Each incoming HTTP request to your protected API or web application inspected by Cybat counts as 1 request. Static assets (images, CSS, fonts) are automatically ignored so you only use your quota on real application traffic. For most startups and mid-size SaaS apps, 1.5M to 5M requests easily covers a full month of traffic.

Do I need to be hosted on Google Cloud to use Cybat?

No! Even if your servers are on AWS, DigitalOcean, Azure, Vercel, or on-premise, you can protect them in 1 click using our Cloudflare Worker integration or by dropping our lightweight SDK into your Node.js, Python, PHP, or Java app.
